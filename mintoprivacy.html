1. Introduction

Welcome to Minto Money Manager ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use our mobile application.

By downloading and using Minto Money Manager, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Handled

Minto Money Manager is designed with a privacy-first, offline-first philosophy:

  • Financial and Transaction Records: All your financial entries, including income, expenses, budgets, categories, and account balances, are stored purely locally on your device in a private SQLite database. We do not host, store, or transmit your financial details to any external cloud server.
  • Biometric Data: If you choose to enable Biometric/Fingerprint App Lock, authentication is performed locally via your device's native operating system (Android BiometricPrompt / iOS LocalAuthentication). The app does not have access to, nor does it store, your fingerprint, face scan, or raw biometric data.
  • On-Device Machine Learning: Smart category predictions and financial insights utilize on-device machine learning models (TensorFlow Lite). Your transaction data is processed locally without communicating with cloud AI services.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The application may integrate third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or provide services:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): We display ads via Google Mobile Ads SDK to support the development and maintenance of the app. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, cookie data, and diagnostic info in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Learn more about how Google uses data from apps by visiting Google's Advertising & Privacy Policies.

4. Device Permissions

To provide core functionalities, the app may request specific system permissions:

  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Required only if you enable app lock for biometric authentication.
  • INTERNET & NETWORK STATE: Used exclusively to fetch advertising content and verify network connectivity for ads.

5. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

Because your financial data is strictly stored locally on your device:

  • Full User Control: You can edit, reset, or delete your transaction data at any time from within the app settings.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application or clearing the application's storage/cache from your device settings permanently removes all locally stored financial records.

6. Children's Privacy

Minto Money Manager does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary action.
